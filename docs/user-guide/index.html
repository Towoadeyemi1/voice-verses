Voice Verses 1.19.3 · for the booth

Voice Verses Guide

How to use every part of Voice Verses, step by step. The numbers in each picture match the numbered steps under it.

1Numbered marker: the step with the same numberRed: Live, what the room seesGreen: Next, what goes up when you press Take

Before you start

Voice Verses listens to the preacher and puts the right Bible verse on your screens by itself. It also runs songs, slides, pictures, videos, countdowns and the order of service.

  • Use the desktop app in church (Mac or Windows). It is one download: listening, the Bibles (KJV, WEB, ASV) and the screens all work without the internet.
  • Red always means Live (what the room sees now). Green always means Next (what goes up when you press Take).
  • Not sure? Nothing you try in Training reaches the room. See Training.

1. Set up your booth (the first time)

The first time Voice Verses opens on a computer, it looks for your microphone, screens and ProPresenter, and asks you to check each one. It takes about five minutes.

Set up your booth: what Voice Verses found

  1. 1The preacher’s microphone. Voice Verses listens to every input and picks the one with sound. Ask someone to speak into the pulpit microphone; the bar moves.
  2. 2Press Looks right if it found the right one. Press Listen again to look again, or Change to pick the input and the desk channel yourself (for example the channel called APOSTLE).
  3. 3Screens. Each output (projector, stream lower third, stage) shows the number of the screen it goes to. Press Identify screens to put a big number on every screen in the room, then Looks right.
  4. 4ProPresenter. If you use it, Voice Verses finds it on this computer or the network: press Yes, that’s it. This only remembers where it is; it does not start sending anything. If you don’t use ProPresenter, press We don’t use ProPresenter.
  5. 5Your services. Tick the services your church holds (Sunday service, Midweek, Friday Night Prayers…). Each one gets a ready-made order of service. Press Looks right.
  6. 6Try the demo plays a two-minute practice sermon, explained step by step. Nothing reaches the room.
  7. 7Finish later closes it. You can open it again any time from Settings → Booth check → Set up your booth.

All four confirmed

  1. 1Try the demo to see Voice Verses at work.
  2. 2Done takes you to the live screen.

2. The live screen at a glance

Everything for the service happens on this one screen. It is the same every Sunday.

The live screen

  1. 1The service. Click its name for the other pages (Plan services, Media, Looks, Screens, Training, Settings).
  2. 2Start listening. Voice Verses starts hearing the preacher. While it listens, a level meter moves and Mute stops it hearing for a moment.
  3. 3Auto / Ask first. Auto: a verse the preacher says outright goes straight up. Ask first: it waits in Next until you press Take.
  4. 4Booth check. Ready (grey tick) or n to check (amber). Click it to see what needs a look.
  5. 5Search (⌘K on a Mac, Ctrl+K on Windows): a verse, a song, a part of the service or an action.
  6. 6Settings (the gear).
  7. 7The order of service. Each part is as wide as its time. Click a part to load it.
  8. 8spirit shows what it will get ready next. Pause (or the P key) stops it doing anything by itself.
  9. 9When I pick a slide: Goes live shows it at once; Waits in Next (the usual choice) puts it in Next first.
  10. 10Live (red): what the room sees now.
  11. 11Take (Space bar): puts Next on the screens.
  12. 12Next (green): what goes up when you press Take.

Before anything is on the screens

  1. 1Start listening when the service begins.
  2. 2Watch the demo: a two-minute sermon and hymn, explained aloud with a pointer.
  3. 3Find a verse, song or item opens Search.
  4. 4Training: short lessons on these real screens.
  5. 5Today’s order: click any part to load its slides.
  6. 6Show a verse: quick buttons for well-known verses.

The library, moving on by time, and spirit

  1. 1Library (top left): the Bible, your songs and your services, to pick from during the service.
  2. 2Move on by time: when a part’s time is up, the next part loads by itself (into Next, or on screen if picking a slide goes live). The sermon, a verse on screen and a song being sung never move on by themselves.
  3. 3Pause (or the P key): spirit stops getting things ready by itself until you press it again.

The library

  1. 1Three tabs: Bible (type a reference; History shows what was on screen), Songs (your song library) and Service (today’s services, coming up and earlier, each part with its slides). Click anything to pick it.
Good to know
  • Several services on the same day sit side by side before the order of service; one click switches between them.
  • The window can be any size: on a wide monitor the library stays open as a column on the left.

3. Watch the demo

The demo plays a short sermon and a hymn through the real listening engine. A voice says what is happening, and a pointer shows where.

The demo explains each step

  1. 1The card says what is happening; the same words are spoken. The speaker button turns the voice off.
  2. 2Stop the demo ends it at any time.

Following the reading

  1. 1The verse being read, large, with the word being read underlined.
  2. 2Heard: the last words Voice Verses heard.
  3. 3Live: the verse went up because the preacher said “John chapter 3 verse 16”.
  4. 4Next: the following verse is ready, in case the preacher reads on. It goes up when this verse ends.

4. Put a verse on the screens yourself

Three steps: find it, check it in Next, press Take.

Search

  1. 1Press ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows), or click the magnifying glass. Type the verse: John 3:16, Psalm 23, Romans 8:28-39.
  2. 2Press Return, or click Show John 3:16.

The verse waits in Next

  1. 1Next shows exactly how it will look on the projector.
  2. 2Press Take (or the Space bar) to put it on the screens.
  3. 3When I pick a slide decides whether picking goes straight to the screens (Goes live) or waits in Next (Waits in Next).
  4. 4The verse also shows large here, with the rest of the chapter underneath to pick from.

On the screens

  1. 1Live (red) now shows John 3:16, and so does the room.
  2. 2Clear (Esc) takes the words off the screens.
  3. 3Logo shows the church logo on every screen.
  4. 4KJV ▾ changes the translation of everything on screen and in Next.
Good to know
  • Press → for the next verse and ← for the one before.

5. Let it listen

Press Start listening and the preacher does the work.

A suggestion

  1. 1The ring shows how much of the verse’s wording was heard.
  2. 2Why it was suggested, in plain words (most of it quoted, in today’s plan, the book was mentioned…).
  3. 3Show now puts it straight on the screens.
  4. 4Use puts it in Next, to check first. The × dismisses it. Click the bar to see every suggestion of the last few minutes.
Good to know
  • Said outright (“John 3:16”, “John chapter 3 verse 16”, “Romans 8 from verse 28”): in Auto it goes straight up; in Ask first it waits in Next.
  • Read aloud: each verse goes up as the reader gets to it, and the next one waits in Next.
  • Named without a verse (“turn to Isaiah 53”): the chapter loads and waits until the preacher reads or names a verse.
  • Quoted or told in their own words: offered as a suggestion. It never goes up by itself unless it is word for word (99% sure).
  • During songs, press ♪ Song so verses stay off the screen unless a reference is said outright.

6. Songs and slides

Songs, announcements and pictures come from the order of service.

Load a part of the service

  1. 1Click the part in the order of service (here, the hymn Holy, Holy, Holy).
  2. 2Its slides appear along the bottom. Click one to pick it; double-click to show it at once.
  3. 3The first slide waits in Next.
  4. 4Press Take (Space) to show it, then Space again for each slide.
  5. 5See all slides shows them as a grid.

A song on the screens

  1. 1Live · V1.1: the slide on the screens, framed red.
  2. 2Next · V1.2: the slide that comes next, framed green.
  3. 3The pencil (Quick edit) fixes a word or changes the look, even during the service.
  4. 4♪ Song: while the church sings, the words follow the singers, and verses stay off the screen.

Quick edit

  1. 1Change the section name, the words or the look, and see how the room will see it. Press Save. For the full designer, press Open the full designer.

Every slide as a grid

  1. 1Show the words large goes back to the normal view.
Good to know
  • The worship leader can call parts of the song out loud: “Verse two!”, “Back to the chorus”. Voice Verses goes there.
  • New words that are in no song are offered as a new song, and kept as a draft afterwards.

7. Countdowns and alerts

Both buttons are at the top of the live screen and every other page.

Countdown

  1. 1Quick start: before the service, until the service starts, a sermon timer (stage only), and more.
  2. 2Length of time (5 minutes) or Until a time (10:00).
  3. 3Message above the numbers, such as “Service begins in”.
  4. 4Show it on: all screens, the projector, the stage monitor or the stream; full screen or small in the corner.
  5. 5Turn yellow with 1 minute left; Blink when yellow and red. It turns red at zero, and the stage shows how far over it is.
  6. 6Start. Several countdowns can run at once, each on its own screens.

Alert: a message over whatever is on screen

  1. 1For the room: nursery, children’s church, car lights, move a car, seats at the front.
  2. 2For the stage only: five minutes, please wrap up, speak up, microphone.
  3. 3Type the child number or number plate (or write any message).
  4. 4Show it on: which screens.
  5. 5Hide it: until you clear it, or after 15 seconds to 2 minutes.
  6. 6Amber and Blink make it stand out. The preview shows how the projector will look; the button under it shows the alert. While one is up, the Alert button turns amber with an × to clear it.

8. Is everything ready?

The booth check looks at the microphone, the screens, ProPresenter, the Bible and today’s service every minute.

The booth check light

  1. 1Ready (grey tick) or n to check (amber). Click it.
  2. 2Each check, with a button that takes you straight to the fix.

Settings → Booth check

  1. 1The booth check at the top of Settings.
  2. 2Set up your booth finds everything again.
Good to know
  • During the service, a problem (a screen unplugged, no sound for 20 seconds) shows as an amber warning under the top bar, with its fix button.

9. Design how things look

A look is the design for one kind of thing on one screen: scripture on the projector, songs on the stream, and so on. Change a look and every slide that uses it changes, on the screens straight away. Open Looks from the page bar.

Looks

  1. 1Three tabs: Design a look, Which screen uses what, and Colour teams and themes. Click a look in the row to design it.
  2. 2Backgrounds: moving backgrounds made by the app, and 40 ready-made samples.
  3. 3Starter looks: ready-made designs to add and change.
  4. 4New look starts one from nothing.

The designer

  1. 1On this slide: click the part to change (Background, Main text, Reference / title, Logo, Transition). Text, Shape and Picture at the bottom add your own layers.
  2. 2Try it with a short verse, a long verse, a song or an announcement, so it works for all of them.
  3. 3Undo and Redo (⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z).
  4. 4Duplicate makes a copy to change. Reset puts a built-in look back as it came.

Change the background

  1. 1Click Background.
  2. 2Choose Colour, Gradient, Moving, Picture or Video. Moving backgrounds are drawn by the app in your team’s colours, with a speed (0 is still). A picture or video can be dimmed and blurred so the words stay readable.

Backgrounds

  1. 1In your colours (they follow your colour team) or Samples (40). Pick one, choose what it goes behind on the projector (Scripture, Songs, Announcements, Countdowns), then press Use.

Starter looks

  1. 1Tabs for Scripture, Songs, Announcements, Countdowns and Lower thirds. Add copies one into your looks; Use on projector puts it to work straight away.

Which screen uses what

  1. 1For each screen (the projector, the lower third for the stream, the stage), choose the look for Scripture, Songs, Announcements and Countdowns.

Colour teams and themes

  1. 1Colour team: five colours every look can use. Change the team and every look changes with it. Change with the month picks the team for the season (Advent purple in December, Easter in April). More colour teams adds Christmas, Lent, Youth and others; New team makes your own. Themes set every look and a colour team in one click (Use this theme).

One slide, its own look

  1. 1In the service editor, open a slide’s ⋮ menu and choose Design…

Designing one slide

  1. 1The same designer, for this slide. At the bottom, choose where it applies: This slide, or every slide of that kind (for example Every announcements slide).
Good to know
  • Move and resize: drag the text box to move it, and its corners to resize it. Pink guide lines show when things line up.
  • The text panel on the right: font, largest size (long verses shrink to fit), bold, italic, underline and highlight, colours (the team’s colours or any), alignment, up or down, line and letter spacing, shadow and outline, a box behind the text, and where it sits.
  • Single words: select words in the text and use the bold, italic, underline, highlight or colour buttons.
  • Transitions: click Transition in the list: dissolve, through black, lift, zoom, push or cut, from 0.2 to 3 seconds. A slide in a service can have its own.
  • During the service: Quick edit (the pencil on a slide, or Edit above Live) changes a slide’s words or look on the spot.
  • Slow computer? Settings → Screens and outputs → Moving backgrounds on this computer can keep them still.

10. Screens: projector, stream and stage

Voice Verses puts each output on its screen by itself, every time. Open Settings → Screens and outputs (or the Screens page).

Screens and outputs

  1. 1Screens and outputs in the sidebar.
  2. 2Click a screen in the picture of the room.
  3. 3Choose what it shows: Projector, Lower third, Stage monitor or Nothing. It opens there by itself, every time.
  4. 4Identify screens puts a big number on every screen.

Screens on the network

  1. 1Click On the network in the picture of the room and switch Share on. Any browser on the church network can then show an output: a smart TV, a tablet on stage, another computer, or OBS and vMix as a browser source. The addresses it shows can be copied; the port is 47100.

NDI® on the network

  1. 1Click NDI on the network. Each output can be an NDI source for vMix, OBS, TriCaster, NDI converters and ProPresenter; the lower third goes see-through. It needs the free NDI Runtime on this computer (NDI Tools, from ndi.video/tools).

Each output, and where it goes

  1. 1Every output with its picture, where it is shown, its screen shape (match the screen, 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, ultrawide or your own size) and Send it somewhere else too: one output can go to a screen and a DeckLink port and the network at once.

Ways to connect a screen

  1. 1Ways to connect a screen: a display cable (HDMI, DisplayPort, USB‑C, AirPlay), Blackmagic DeckLink (SDI straight into the switcher; choose the video format your switcher uses and press Show test colours), the network, ProPresenter and NDI. Each says whether it is ready.

The stage monitor’s own settings

  1. 1On the stage monitor, move the mouse and click the gear. Start from a layout (Classic, Large words, Preacher, Singers or High contrast), then change colours and sizes.
Good to know
  • Esc on an output that sits on your own screen turns it off.
  • If a screen is unplugged, its output comes back by itself when it is plugged in again, and the booth check says so meanwhile.
  • An HDMI-to-SDI converter on a spare video output works too, like any other screen.

11. ProPresenter: three ways to connect

ProPresenter is optional: Voice Verses’ own screens keep working whichever way you choose, and nothing is sent while you practise. Open Settings → ProPresenter and choose how the words get there.

Way 1: a slide with linked text (recommended for most churches)

  1. 1Choose A slide with linked text. Your team keeps control: they put the slide up and take it down, and each screen keeps its own look.
  2. 2Send the words to ProPresenter: on for the service.
  3. 3Also song words: song slides go there too, if you want.
  4. 4The folder where Voice Verses writes the words. Show the files opens it. If ProPresenter is on another computer, press Choose another folder and pick a folder both computers can see (a shared folder, or a Dropbox, iCloud or OneDrive folder).
  5. 5Write a test verse writes John 3:16 now (even with sending off), so you can link the text box and see it. Clear it empties it.

Way 1, in ProPresenter (once)

  1. 1The page lists the six steps: make a slide called “Voice Verses” with a text box; select the text box → Format → Linked Text → File → choose verse.txt (or verse-and-reference.txt; a second box can show reference.txt); press Write a test verse here and the box shows John 3:16; give it the look you want on each screen; in the service, put the slide up when the reading starts, and its words follow Voice Verses.

Way 2: a slide with Voice Verses’ picture

  1. 1Choose A slide with Voice Verses’ picture. ProPresenter shows exactly what Voice Verses draws: verses, songs, slides and countdowns, with the same look on every screen.
  2. 2Switch on Send the projector over NDI. This computer needs the free NDI Runtime (NDI Tools, from ndi.video/tools).
  3. 3In ProPresenter, once: Settings → Inputs, add an NDI input and choose “… (Voice Verses Projector)”; then make a slide whose background is that input, and put it up when the reading starts.

Way 3: a message over everything

  1. 1Choose A message over everything. It is the quickest to set up, but the message shows over every screen whose Look has Messages on.
  2. 2Switch Send verses on. Under it, ProPresenter on your network lists every ProPresenter found; press Connect beside yours. Voice Verses makes the “Voice Verses” message with a lower-third theme.

Way 3: what ProPresenter shows

  1. 1Also on ProPresenter’s stage screens sends a stage message as well.
  2. 2Send a test verse, then Clear it.
  3. 3Why isn’t it on the screens? lists what ProPresenter says (the message, its layers and the Look on each screen) and what to change.
Good to know
  • Which way? Linked text if your team runs ProPresenter and wants its own looks and control. The NDI picture if you want Voice Verses’ look (and songs and slides) inside ProPresenter. The message only for a quick start.
  • Clear (Esc), the Logo, or a song or slide going up takes Voice Verses’ verse off ProPresenter (linked text: the files are emptied).
  • With the message: if someone presses Hide in ProPresenter, Voice Verses stops sending and offers Send again.
  • ProPresenter not found? In ProPresenter: Settings → Network → Enable Network, on the same network. Or type its address (Not in the list? → Type the address yourself).
  • The booth check checks the way you chose: the folder, the NDI picture, or the message.

12. Other apps and devices

A Stream Deck, a phone, or your church’s own apps (a dashboard, Planning Center, a captioning tool) can use Voice Verses over the church network. Each one is paired once, with a code shown on this computer, so only devices someone in the booth paired can use it. It needs the desktop app, and it works without the internet.

Turn it on

  1. 1Settings → Other apps and devices: switch it on. Switch it off and no other app can use Voice Verses (the network screens have their own switch).
  2. 2The address other apps use. Most find Voice Verses on the network by themselves.
  3. 3Control the screens: on, the device you pair next can show verses, Take, go forward or back, Clear and show the logo. Off, it can only see what’s on and follow what happens.
  4. 4Send in services: on for an app that plans your services (the CCCG Dashboard, Planning Center, your church’s own app). It can send a whole service for any day: the order, songs with their credits, the scriptures expected and announcements.
  5. 5Show a pairing code.

Pair a device

  1. 1Type these six digits in the other app. The code works once, for five minutes.
  2. 2Stop if you change your mind.

What is paired

  1. 1Each paired device, what it may do, and when it last used Voice Verses. Places it sends events to are listed under it, with Stop sending.
  2. 2Remove ends that device’s access at once. To pair it again, show a new code.
Good to know
  • A remote Take does exactly what the Take button does. While you practise in Training, other apps can’t change the screens.
  • A service sent in appears in Services on its day, marked From and the app’s name, and you see a message when it arrives. Sending it again updates it. Its scriptures expected are in the sermon, so detection finds them more easily, and loading them puts the first verse in Next.
  • A service saved as a file by another app (a service package, .json) imports with Services → Import.
  • For the people who make the apps: the page links to the guide for developers (the API, its events, webhooks and the service package).

13. Settings, page by page

Open Settings with the gear. Everything saves as you change it; there is no Save button to forget. Screens and ProPresenter have their own sections above.

Features: what Voice Verses does by itself

  1. 1Features in the sidebar.
  2. 2Choose Essentials, Recommended or Everything.
  3. 3Or switch each feature on or off. A verse said outright always goes up, whatever is off.

Listening: the microphone

  1. 1Listening in the sidebar.
  2. 2Input: the microphone or the sound desk’s USB feed.
  3. 3Sound check: speak and the bar moves. If the desk sends many channels, click the preacher’s channel (its meter moves when they speak).

Listening: how speech is heard

  1. 1How speech is heard: On this computer (recommended: offline and private), The browser (the web version only) or OpenAI (over the internet, with your own key).

Listening: test a recording, and the AI assistant

Test with a recording: choose a recorded service (MP3, M4A or WAV) and see every verse Voice Verses would have shown. AI verse assistant (optional): with Claude, OpenAI, Ollama or LM Studio on your own computer, or another compatible server, it also recognises verses quoted or described instead of named. Keys are kept in the computer’s own keychain, never in the web version.

Bible translations

  1. 1Bible in the sidebar.
  2. 2On this computer: KJV, ASV, WEB, or your own. Always works, no internet needed.
  3. 3Over the internet: ESV, NLT and API.Bible translations (NKJV, NIV, CSB…), each with a free key from its publisher.

Songs and CCLI

  1. 1Songs and CCLI in the sidebar.
  2. 2Your CCLI licence number, shown small on each song’s first slide with its writers and copyright.

Logo

  1. 1Logo in the sidebar. Choose a picture; a transparent PNG looks best. The Logo button shows it on every screen.

Library and backup

  1. 1Library and backup in the sidebar. Choose folder inside iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive to back up your services and share them between computers.

Desktop app

  1. 1Desktop app in the sidebar. Updates at the top: the version on this computer and whether a newer one is out. Voice Verses looks when it opens and every 4 hours; Check now looks at once. On Windows it downloads by itself, then Restart to update (or it installs when you quit). On a Mac, Download puts the new installer in Downloads, then Open the installer: quit Voice Verses, drag it to Applications and choose Replace. A new version also shows beside Desktop app in this list, with a message. Below: downloads for Mac (Apple chip or Intel) and Windows, each one installer with everything, the offline speech engine included.

14. spirit: let it run the service

spirit learns how your church runs its service, gets the next thing ready, and earns your trust one move at a time. It never puts a verse up that wasn’t said.

When spirit gets something wrong

  1. 1spirit put Blessed Assurance in Next because the last slide of the song before it went up. Next says why.
  2. 2Not this tells spirit it was wrong.
  3. 3Say why in one tap: Wrong part, Not now or Never for these words. spirit remembers.

How spirit runs

  1. 1How spirit runs in the sidebar.
  2. 2How much spirit does: Off; Ready in Next (it gets things ready and you press Take); Hands-free (only for moves that have been right 10 times in a row).
  3. 3Your last service: what spirit got right, what you chose, what you corrected.
  4. 4What spirit knows.

What spirit does

  1. 1What spirit does in the sidebar.
  2. 2Spoken cues: moments and sermon points.
  3. 3Before and after the service: the countdown into the next part, and more coming soon.
  4. 4Songs: the next song when one ends.

Cues: teach spirit your church’s words

  1. 1Cues in the sidebar. At the top, fill in the sentence: When the preacher says “as the ushers make their way” get Offering ready, then press Teach spirit.
  2. 2Prayer: the phrases spirit already knows for prayer (“let us pray”, “bow your heads”…).
  3. 3Giving: “tithes and offerings”, “receive the offering”…
  4. 4Welcome, and below it every other moment of the service, each with its phrases.
Good to know
  • spirit also learns by watching: when the same words keep coming before the same part, it suggests them as a new cue.
  • If spirit gets something wrong, press Not this beside Next and say why (wrong part, not now, never for these words). It remembers.
  • Pause (P) on the live screen hands everything back to you at once.
  • When listening stops, How it went shows spirit’s score, with Just this once to undo a correction.

15. Plan your services

Open Services from the page bar (or the service name on the live screen).

Services

  1. 1The page bar: Live, Services, Media, Looks, Screens, Training, Settings. Pick a day in the calendar; its services show under it.
  2. 2New service.
  3. 3Import a service saved to a file (from another computer or church).
  4. 4Copy for next Sunday makes a fresh copy.
  5. 5Save to a file, to send or keep.
  6. 6Present opens it to run; Edit opens the editor.

Templates and ready-made orders

  1. 1New template: your own order of service for a type of service, from a ready-made order, a service you have, or nothing.
  2. 2Ready-made orders: one for every type of service. Use for the chosen day, or Make it a template.

The service editor

  1. 1Type of service (Sunday service, Communion Sunday, Friday Night Prayers…). spirit learns each type separately.
  2. 2Save as a template keeps this service as a starting point for next time.
  3. 3Present runs it.
  4. 4Save.

Add to a part

  1. 1Click a part in the order, then Add to “…”: Scripture, Song, Announcement, Picture, Video or Blank. Drag parts and slides to reorder; each part has a timer for the timeline.
  2. 2Make with AI turns the bulletin or sermon notes into slides.
  3. 3From a file adds a presentation or the sermon notes to the section (or drop the file on a section).

Add from a file: a presentation or the sermon notes

  1. 1Scriptures written in the notes, each with its first words. They go in as scripture slides, so listening finds them more easily (a verse still goes up by itself only when it is said outright), and loading them puts the first in Next.
  2. 2A chapter alone (“like Noah in Genesis 6”) is often mentioned in passing, so it starts unticked. Tick it if it is read.
  3. 3Points: the headings and numbered lines. Each becomes a slide.
  4. 4The rest of the notes go to the stage monitor during the sermon, for the preacher. Never to the projector or the stream.
  5. 5Add puts them in the section as parts. Then Save the service.

Make slides with AI (optional)

  1. 1Announcements from a bulletin or Sermon points from notes: paste the words, press Write the slides, check them, then Add slides to this item. It needs an AI provider (Settings → Listening → AI verse assistant); it keeps your dates, times and places as written.
Good to know
  • A part can have its own countdown that starts with its first slide (the Countdown before the service does this).
  • Remove samples takes away the sample services and songs the app came with.
  • From a file, a presentation: a PDF comes in as pictures, exactly as made (Keynote and Google Slides: save as PDF), or as words in your church’s look. A PowerPoint (.pptx) comes in as words and pictures in your look, with its speaker notes for the stage; for an exact copy, save it as PDF first. Untick any pages you don’t want. Scriptures written on the slides go in too.
  • Notes on the stage monitor: a slide’s own notes, or its part’s, show on the stage monitor under the slide while it is up (the notes button on a part or slide). Never on the projector or the stream.

16. Media, the Screens page and Presenter

Media

17. Training

Short lessons on the real screens, in practice: nothing reaches the projector, the stream, the stage or ProPresenter.

Training

  1. 1Training in the page bar (or the Training button on the live screen).
  2. 2Show me: Voice Verses does each step, with a pointer and a voice.
  3. 3Let me try: you do each step; it waits and checks.
  4. 4Watch: the demo, explained aloud.

Show me

  1. 1The step, on a card beside the control. Back, Pause, and Let me try this step.
  2. 2The speaker turns the voice on or off. The × (or Esc) leaves the lesson.
  3. 3End practice on the violet band stops the lesson.

Let me try

  1. 1The lit control is the one to click.
  2. 2Click somewhere else and it says what you clicked, and nothing changes. After two tries it offers Show me this step.

18. What the room sees

The projector

Scripture, songs and slides in their look, with countdowns and alerts on top.

The stage monitor

The current verse or slide, what is coming next, the clock and any stage countdown or alert.

The stream lower third

The verse on a green key colour for the stream switcher. Over NDI it goes see-through.

When something goes wrong

If…Do this
Nothing happens when the preacher speaksLook at the booth check light. In Settings → Listening, check the input and speak: the bar must move. With a desk feed, click the preacher’s channel.
A screen is black or on the wrong displaySettings → Screens and outputs → Identify screens, then click the screen and choose what it shows.
A wrong verse was suggestedPress × on the suggestion. Suggestions never go up by themselves. Switch to Ask first if you want every verse to wait in Next.
spirit got the wrong part readyPress Not this beside Next and say why. Press P to pause spirit.
ProPresenter’s message covers its screensChoose A slide with linked text in Settings → ProPresenter: your team then puts the verse slide up and takes it down. For now, Hide the message in ProPresenter: Voice Verses stops sending until you choose Send again.
Something went up by mistakePress Esc to clear, or the Logo button.
You want to practiseTraining → Your first Sunday → Let me try. Nothing reaches the room.
