The screen changes with the service

Preaching shows the reading and suggestions; worship shows the song's parts; announcements show the slides. The app switches by itself from what it hears, and the running order lets you jump.

ProPresenter: the same panels all the time; you find the right one.

The service runs across the top

Every part of the service with its time, what is on now, and how long is left. Click any part to load it.

ProPresenter: playlists in a side list, with no times or progress.

One search for everything

Verses, words from a verse, songs, service items and actions such as “countdown 5” or “clear”, from one box.

ProPresenter: separate Bible, library and media searches.

Live and Next, not Preview and Program

What is on screen, and what comes up next and when (“at the end of verse 9”). Space sends it. Red only ever means live; green only ever means next.

ProPresenter: slide-by-slide clicking, no idea what comes next.

Scripture you can read from the booth

The verse being read, large, lit word by word, with the passage's verses as numbered steps. Suggestions show how sure the match is.

ProPresenter: no listening, no follower, no confidence.

Real thumbnails, calm design

Slides drawn with their real backgrounds and fonts. One accent colour, a clear type scale, light and dark looks, and quiet motion only where it tells you something.

ProPresenter: dense, busy chrome built for experts.
