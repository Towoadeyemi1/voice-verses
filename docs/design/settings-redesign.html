Scripture

Follow the readingWhile a passage is read aloud, each verse goes up as the reader reaches it.
A chapter alone waits for its verse“Somewhere in Isaiah 53” loads the chapter and waits for the verse to be read or named.
Verses quoted without a referenceOffered as suggestions. Shown by themselves only when quoted word for word.
Stories in the preacher’s own words“Zacchaeus climbed a sycamore tree” offers Luke 19. Always a suggestion.
“Help me find it”When the preacher asks for help, everything just said is searched at once.
Predictive Next“Paul says the same in Romans” puts the likely verse in Next, waiting for Take.

Worship

Recognise songs from your libraryWhen a song you have is being sung, it is offered so you can switch to it.
The worship leader’s cues“One more time”, “go to the bridge”, “back to the chorus” move the slides.
Singing something newWords sung that are in no song are offered, and can be kept as a draft song.

The service

A part’s countdown starts with it“Countdown” before the service starts counting when its slide goes up.
Moment autopilot Coming“Let us pray”, “as the ushers come forward”: the order of service moves to that part, into Next.
Sermon points on cue Coming“Point number two…”: the preacher’s words become a point slide, ready in Next.

Safety

Booth checkA “Ready” light on the live screen, with a Fix button for anything that isn’t.
Warnings during the serviceIf the microphone goes quiet or a screen stops, a warning appears on the live screen.
