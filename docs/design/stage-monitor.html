Stage TV · 55 inch · 1920 × 1080

What changes

    Readable from (55-inch TV)

    About 1 cm of letter height for every 1.5 m of distance, the rule for signs. People on a platform stand about 3–5 m from a stage TV.

    Where Next goes

    Along the bottom, Next gets the full width, so the next lines of a song fit in big letters. A church that wants only what is on can switch it off.
